Plastic World Model Agent · Complete Findings Record

Memory as changed dynamics, not retrieved content.

A fourteen-day autonomous research project set out to make a language model metabolize corrections into its own weights. This is the exhaustive record of what was tried, what was rejected, and why. Three of four headline claims fell under controls. The mechanism the thesis was built on is refuted from four directions. What survives is retrieval — and two untested bets.

01

The thesis & the verdict

The north-star metric was behavior_delta_per_byte on held-out probes after raw traces are deleted. A correction arrives, changes the model's fast weights, consolidates into slow weights, and the original text is forgotten — yet the behavior survives. That is the one experiment that distinguishes "changed dynamics" from "retrieval with extra steps."

The first twelve days (an LFM2.5-1.2B model through llama.cpp) produced genuinely good infrastructure and four confident headline claims — on an evaluation that had co-evolved with the system it measured. A three-day remediation program froze the eval, removed the leakage, and re-adjudicated everything under pre-registration. The table below is the result.

Everything that tried to change the model's internal processing from accumulated experience failed. The thesis is not refuted — but the cheapest formalization of it, changed-dynamics = additive bias, is dead.

03

The mechanism graveyard

Every mechanism proposed for "changed dynamics," with the number that killed it. These are rejected theories, not failed code — each was a real hypothesis about where memory could live.

Activation steering from accumulated experience Refuted ×3cvec · S2.4 · research/02
ClaimEmbed each correction, accumulate into a Hebbian warm state, emit as a residual-stream vector; the model behaves in the direction of its experience.
Why rejectedAveraging correction embeddings amplifies their common mode (format, register), not the fact. The vector grows louder, not more directed. S2.4: control-word logits rose 2.92 vs target 1.60; clamping the norm to a fixed budget dropped the gain to 0.566 < the old 0.627; survival ratio 0.354. It was shouting, not steering.
CorollaryA constant residual vector is an unconditional rank-1 bias. It can carry posture (style, domain-prior) but structurally cannot express "when 'log' appears here, say journal." cvec-forced tokens stalled at rank ~47,000 while prefix/KV hit rank 1.
Mid-layer hidden states hold extractable concepts Refuted, both architecturesGoal 2 · S1.4 · lane_03
ClaimSemantic intent forms at intermediate depth; a probe reading layer L clusters by concept better than the final layer.
Why rejectedAcross all 24 Qwen layers the final-layer mean-pool silhouette (0.1753) beat the best mid-layer (0.0922) — gap −0.083, threshold was +0.10. LFM2.5's gap of +0.058 also fell short. The llama.cpp keyhole (final layer only) had hidden this for a week; the HF substrate saw every layer and refuted it cleanly.
KV-slot injection forces exact-token recall Refuted as-specifiedmechanism validatedGoal 1 · S1.3 · research/09
ClaimA fact encoded once into KV entries and spliced into a later probe's cache forces rank-1 recall with no visible prefix.
Why rejectedOnly 1 of 3 facts reached rank 1 (threshold 2). 2× K/V norm scaling was catastrophic — ranks blew to 2106 / 99 / 181.
What survivedSpliced KV entries are rank-for-rank identical to a text prefix at zero visible-token cost, and the pre-blank splice position fixed the worst case (rook 4 → 0). This is the one positive mechanism the whole project produced. Injection latency 183.9 ms mean on CPU torch.
A two-component loop closes: correction → fast weights → held-out behavior RefutedS2.1 · research/11
ClaimHFDriver + a fast-weight cortex + a hippocampus replay buffer, nothing else, produce measurable held-out behavior change that compounds with corrections K.
Why rejectedloop_delta_holdout = 0.0000 at K=N across 5 seeds; compounding ρ = nan. A transient bump at K=4 (0.133) collapsed to zero at K=8: the 48-token prefix budget holds ~3.7 corrections, so facts crowd out instead of compounding. The content channel works only while a fact survives the budget.
cvec posture works at a calibrated amplitude on the new substrate Refuted at every amplitudeS2.1
Why rejectedRaw warm-state cvecs (combined norm ~140) collapsed generation into a repeated garbage token — the S2.4 pathology reborn as a bug. Even clamped to combined norm 1.0 they still corrupted probes on Qwen. Posture disabled pending calibration. Two independent agents diagnosed this identically — the strongest confirmation the project produced.
The inference substrate: llama.cpp + a hybrid model Rejected (root cause)Audit finding 1
Why rejectedllama-cpp-python provides neither KV writes (Goal 1) nor mid-layer reads (Goal 2) — it sabotaged both goals from day one. Choosing LFM2.5, a conv+attention hybrid, made it worse: KV snapshots ballooned to 348,768 bytes per 15-token fact because conv1d recurrence state is captured too. The escape hatch (bench_hf_cpu.py) existed in-repo and went untaken for twelve days. For research about intervening in model internals, pick the most transparent substrate, not the fastest.

Instrument failures the remediation itself surfaced

  • The validator broke silently. The pre-registered holdout split hashed all 8 stage-0 probes into dev — an empty holdout the split's own validator defines as an error (a ~6% unlucky outcome, missed because the health check tested a different fraction). Caught only because a "REFUTE" looked too hollow.
  • The harness overwrote the record. The ablation runner hardcoded its report path to a historical log and silently rewrote it on every test run — caught as unexplained working-tree churn.
  • A parallel-agent seam never fired. HFDriver.load()'s default had never worked: one agent exported HF_MODEL_ID, another imported DEFAULT_MODEL_ID, and try/except ImportError ate the mismatch. First exercised — and fixed — on first real use.
The working agreement that came out of it The optimizing loop never touches the measuring instrument. Metrics, thresholds, baselines, and episodes are frozen per version and changed only by explicit human decision with a version bump. "Breakthrough" requires ablation + trajectory + seeds. Nulls and refutations are logged as prominently as wins.

Curriculum design

Six leveled stages, each targeting a specific organ and a specific claim. The design intent is a ladder: grounding → paraphrase transfer → conditional scope → dialog → consolidation stress → cross-domain. The probe categories map one-to-one onto what each mechanism must prove.

StageWhat it teachesTargetsEp.
0 · GroundingSingle ambiguous word, one correction, same request repeated ("'log' means the captain's journal")Cortex fast weights20
1 · TransferNew requests using the corrected sense — the paraphrase-generalization axis where lookup fails and a learned mapping can winHippocampus replay20
2 · ScopeSame word, second sense in a new context ("'log' here means system error log"); each sense used only in its own contextImmune + Identity13
3 · DialogMulti-turn: a correction then a context-dependent follow-up — full metabolism across turnsAll organs4
4 · ConsolidationMany corrections then an explicit consolidate(); memory measured before & afterReplay + compression10
5 · Cross-domainSame word maps to different senses by domain; consolidate_before=TrueCritic + Identity + Immune6

Design decisions of record

  • Ambiguous words avoid the cortex's baseline vocabulary. Every taught word is validated against PlasticCortex.BASELINE so a "correct" answer can't be a prior the model already held.
  • Scope probes are the template-killer. A keyword or classifier engine dies on Stage 2 — the same word must resolve two ways by context alone. This is the guardrail against "cortex collapses into classifier + template."
  • Deterministic dev/holdout split by stable hash. split_probes(fraction=0.3, salt="v2") — primaries scored on holdout only, with a non-empty-holdout guarantee added after the degenerate stage-0 split was caught.
v2 → v2.1 expansion · Jul 3 To close power gaps for the two remaining bets, stages 0/1/2 grew under a human-approved version bump: 12 new ambiguous words, a 40-probe stage-1 transfer battery, and 4 adversarial scope probes whose surface verb cues the wrong domain ("Roll out the model." → fashion model). Existing episodes were never modified; the manifest was regenerated; every prior number stays comparable.

Goal 3, closed honestly research/15 — "wire the survivors' outputs to tensors" — is vacuous: no component earned KEEP, so there is nothing to wire. The only working component is a reranker, and it is retrieval by honest label. The thesis's central claim — that changed dynamics, not retrieval, does the work — is unsupported by every controlled measurement to date.
The honest positive The minimal prefix organism is not uniformly dead: it lifts Stage 4 holdout to 0.250 (zero variance, vanilla 0.000) and Stage 5 to 0.556 (vanilla 0.333). The content channel works where the fact fits the budget. What fails is compounding.

What remains — the two bets

Exactly two mechanisms consistent with all evidence are untested. Both became possible only when the substrate gained gradients. Both run on eval v2.1, end in the same forgetting 2×2, and are scored with honest per-byte and per-context-token accounting.

research/18 — Consolidation as context distillation Pre-registered
BetPlasticity in LM weights. Each correction becomes a transient per-fact prefix on a frozen teacher; a LoRA student is KL-distilled to match the teacher's behavior without the prefix. Then delete prefix and traces — if behavior survives in the adapter, memory has become changed dynamics.
Why it can winIt escapes S2.1's budget-eviction failure by construction: facts accumulate in weights, not in a token budget. A teacher-validity gate (≥ 0.2 dev lift) with a single chat-template fallback keeps a substrate failure from masquerading as a refutation.
research/19 — The LM as language organ Pre-registered
BetPlasticity outside the LM. Treat the frozen model as a sensory-motor organ — perception in, articulation out — and put the plasticity in a ≤64k-param head over frozen embeddings that learns request → sense, with an abstain path for untaught inputs.
Why it can winThe frozen LM supplies linguistic generalization (paraphrases land near each other); the head supplies the trainable, deletable mapping. The reranker's zero-variance wins (S0 +.667, S4 +.250) are the bar it must clear on transfer — the axis where exemplar rerank structurally cannot generalize.
Judgement day Both bets end in one head-to-head table: 18 vs 19 vs the retrieval baseline vs vanilla, same eval, same forgetting test, same accounting. If both refute, the recorded conclusion is that retrieval is the architecture — and the north star, as originally formulated, was measuring the wrong thing. That would be a clean negative result about a whole class of memory architectures: not the outcome hoped for, but a publishable one, earned honestly.
